# Radiation Science Hub

Welcome to my research portfolio. I am a radiation scientist working at the intersection
of nuclear physics and medical application — developing tools, tracers, and techniques
that make radiation work for us.

---

## Research Focus

| Area | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Radiopharmaceutical Chemistry** | Designing compounds that deliver radioactive payloads precisely to target tissues |
| **Gamma Spectroscopy** | Characterizing radioactive materials through their gamma emission signatures |
| **Radiation Detection Instrumentation** | Building and calibrating detectors for accurate measurement of ionizing radiation |
| **Nuclear Decay Analysis** | Quantitative study of decay chains, half-lives, and emission profiles |
| **Targeted Alpha Therapy** | Exploring alpha-emitting radionuclides as agents for cancer treatment |
| **Radiotracer Design** | Developing radiolabeled molecules for imaging and therapeutic applications |
